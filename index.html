GYM

gym is a short form of gymnasium, which is a dedicated building, club, or large room equipped with weights, machines, and tools for physical exercise, strength training, and cardio workouts.

abs workout

is the short term for the abdominal muscles located in the front and sides of your trunk between your chest and pelvis. In the gym, training your abs focuses on building muscle size and reducing body fat to make these muscles visible (often called a "six-pack").

bodybuilding

A bodybuilder is a person who regularly performs specialized resistance training and weightlifting exercises in a gym to enlarge, sculpt, and develop their muscle mass (hypertrophy)
